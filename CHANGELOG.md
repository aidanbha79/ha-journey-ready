# Changelog

## 0.1.0, unreleased

- Calendar, traffic and work-only train/Park & Ride planner.
- UI configuration, sensors, actions and persisted reminders.
- Fuel economy, fares, weather preferences and request budgets.
- Phone Yes/No, Waze links and home-only Alexa support.
- Background local Fuel Finder adapter and explicit fallback.
- Provider, planner, selector, lifecycle and packaging tests.

Accounts, routes and Echo responses require commissioning. Direct trains only; bus ride duration/fallback schedules are configured estimates. Live ticket pricing is not implemented.
